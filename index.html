Welcome to R.K. CLUB — An interactive 30-Day Science, Innovation, and Social Awareness Platform for ambitious students and youth across India.

Our Core Aim & Mission

Social Awareness & Self-Funded Student Innovation

Our primary aim is to spread Social Awareness among students and society while helping youth who have a strong interest in Science and Technology move forward in life. We organize grand quizzes and award major prizes specifically so that winners who want to build a new invention, science project, or innovation never have to ask their parents or family for money—they can fund and build their ideas independently using their prize amount!

What We Cover (Explore Our 5 Pillars)
1

Science and Innovation

Practical scientific experiments, real-world physics models, and turning innovative concepts into working prototypes.

2

Space and Astronomy

4 to 6 feet rocket models (SLV, ASLV, PSLV, GSLV), satellite technology, telescopes, and space exploration.

3

Robotics and AI

Hands-on introduction to Robotics, smart sensors, automation, and Artificial Intelligence applications.

4

Cyber Safety and Social Awareness

Digital safety, protection against cyber fraud, responsible internet usage, and positive social awareness.

5

Creativity and New Ideas

Encouraging original thinking, creative problem-solving, and student-led grassroots inventions.
